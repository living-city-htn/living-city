# Role Playbook: 3D

You own how the city looks in moments 1, 2, 4, and 5. Cartoon miniature is a requirement, not a preference. Read docs/01 sections 8.11 and 8.12 before touching a material. Moment 4's visual change is the pitch; the "event look" is yours.

Owned packages: `packages/modeling`, `apps/web/scene`. Fixtures you produce: `taxonomy.v1.json`, `plans.mock.json`. Slots come from the Map owner as data; you only render into them.

Read fully: docs/04 (especially sections 3 and 8), docs/01 sections 8.7, 8.11, 8.12, docs/02 sections 4.5, 10, and 11, docs/03 sections 3.2 and 5.3 to 5.4.

## Your contract

How you build it is yours. The inputs and outputs below are not. If an input is late, use the fallback in the table, keep the interface, and swap in the real thing when it lands.

Inputs:

| Input | From | Shape | If it is late |
|---|---|---|---|
| `city.json` | Map, Stage 0 | `CommunityGeo[]` + `BlockLayout` | Three hand-typed polygons |
| `slots.json` | Map, Stage 1 | 3 slots per block | Three points per block at fixed offsets from the centroid |
| `CommunityPlan` per block | Pipeline, Stage 2 | docs/03 section 3.2 | `plans.mock.json`, which you own |
| Placements for the viewer | Game via Product, Stage 2 | `{community_id, slot_id, item_tag}[]` | A hardcoded list |
| `contracts` package | Pipeline, Stage 0 | Zod types | Type against docs/03 by hand |

Outputs:

| Output | Shape | Who needs it | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset manifest and `taxonomy.v1.json` | Every enum in docs/03 section 5.3 mapped to an asset or alias; 6 shop items with price tiers | Pipeline, Civic (shop) | Gate 0 |
| `plans.mock.json` and the festival plan | Valid `CommunityPlan`s, one per distinct archetype plus the loud one | Product, Pipeline (preset route) | Gate 0 |
| `<CityScene>` component | Props: `city`, `plans`, `placements`, `mode` (public or mine), `selectedId`, `planningIds`. Events: `blockHover(id)`, `blockSelect(id)`, `blockPick(id, point)`, `slotTap(communityId, slotId)` | Product | Stage 1 with mocks, Stage 2 real |
| Deterministic block rendering | Same plan and seed give the same block; rebuild animates | Demo moments 1, 2, 4, 5 | Gate 1 |
| The event look | A plan with the festive fields lights the block up visibly from across a room | Demo moment 4 | Gate 1 |

## Not building
A lot-grid algorithm beyond a clipped regular grid. A slot generator (Map provides slots as data). Idle animation beyond people. Outline post-processing. More than 4 or 5 distinct archetypes, more than 3 effects, weather, incident markers before Stage 4 item 5. See docs/04 sections 3 and 6.

## Suggested checklists

The stages below are one reasonable way to meet the contract. Reorder or replace steps freely as long as the outputs land by their gates.

### Stage 0
- [ ] CC0 low-poly glTF pack under the caps (8 to 12 buildings, 10 to 15 decorations and vegetation, a few people), draco-compressed, license committed.
- [ ] Asset manifest: every enum value in docs/03 section 5.3 maps to an asset or to an alias of a distinct one. Mark the 6 shop items with a price tier.
- [ ] `taxonomy.v1.json` generated from the manifest, with `zoning_defaults`.
- [ ] `plans.mock.json`: one plan per distinct archetype (4 or 5), validated, plus the "festival plan" for the demo block, tuned to be the loudest thing in the city.

### Stage 1
- [ ] Placement engine on a clipped grid: regular grid over the block's bbox, keep cells whose center is inside the polygon, shrink slightly from edges. Assign cells deterministically by seed: buildings from the six-category composition and density, heights from the profile with jitter, vegetation on the rest, one plaza cell near the centroid for crowds and stage, decorations by prominence on edge cells, hero on the plaza. That is the whole algorithm; do not refine it before Gate 3.
- [ ] Cartoon rendering: flat materials with vertex colors, 4 real palettes, raised slab (bevel optional), one directional light with soft shadows, gradient background. People walk in place or along a short loop; nothing else animates idle.
- [ ] The event look: when a plan carries `mood: festive`, `string_lights`, `stage`, `music_notes`, and warm accents, the block visibly lights up with a crowd cluster and floating notes. Test that a calm plan and the event plan on the same block are obviously different from across a room.
- [ ] Rebuild: fade or scale-in over half a second. Lights come on with it.
- [ ] Placements render into the slots from `slots.json`. Slot markers visible in my-city mode, tappable on a phone.
- [ ] Instancing from the start. Capped counts. Reduced-detail mode only if a phone drops below 30 fps.

Gate 1 criteria you own: every mock archetype looks different; the event look is obviously loud; a mock placement renders in a slot.

### Stage 2
- [ ] Real plans on the hand-drawn city. Guard against unknown values anyway.
- [ ] Rebuild only the block whose plan changed.
- [ ] Emit `blockHover`, `blockSelect`, `blockPick(id, point)`, `slotTap(communityId, slotId)`. The scene renders app state and never owns it.

Gate 2 criteria you own: moments 1, 2, 4, 5 look right on the deployed build on a phone.

### Stage 3
- [ ] Frame rate on the demo laptop and the demo phone. Reduced-detail mode if needed.
- [ ] Rehearsals: watch moment 4 from the back of the room and adjust the event look until it lands.

### Stage 4 (only after Gate 3, in docs/04 order)
- [ ] Hero blocks: hero assets, extra palette sets, better people.
- [ ] Remaining blocks up to the cap with aliased archetypes.
- [ ] One weather effect from a fixture.
- [ ] Incident marker on the demo block by status.

### Stage 5
- [ ] Confirm frame rate on the frozen build.
